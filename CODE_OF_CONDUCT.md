## 🤝 Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a **harassment-free** and **inclusive experience** for everyone, regardless of:

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Age, gender, body size, disability, race, ethnicity  
🌍 Nationality, religion, level of experience, education, socio-economic status  
🏳️‍🌈 Sexual identity and orientation

---

### 🌟 Our Standards

#### ✔️ Positive behavior includes:
- Kindness, empathy, and respectful communication
- Embracing diverse opinions and feedback
- Owning mistakes and growing from them
- Focusing on what's best for the **community as a whole**

#### ❌ Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Use of sexualized language or unwanted advances
- Insults, trolling, personal/political attacks
- Harassment (public or private)
- Sharing private info without permission
- Any inappropriate behavior in a professional setting

---

### 🛡️ Enforcement

Community leaders will:
- Enforce standards fairly and respectfully
- Take appropriate corrective action when needed
- Have the right to edit/remove content that violates the code
- Ensure the privacy of those reporting issues

🔎 **Scope:** This code applies to all community spaces (public and private), including GitHub, emails, social media, and events.

📧 **Report issues to:**  
**designweb.azadeh@gmail.com**  
Reports are confidential and taken seriously.
